Privacy Policy

Application: BEYOND  |  Effective Date: August 30, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BEYOND ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (Package Name: com.mrhideo.beyond). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (via Google Sign-In)

When you choose to sign in to BEYOND using Google Sign-In (powered by Google Identity Services / Credential Manager), we receive basic profile information authorized by your Google account:

  • Name & Display Name: Used to personalize your experience and show your profile in the app.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed within your in-app account card.
  • Firebase User ID (UID): A unique cryptographic string generated upon authentication, used strictly to associate and isolate your cloud data.

B. User Content (Notes & Whiteboard Drawings)

  • Page Notes: Text, checklists, and document contents created by you.
  • Whiteboard Drawings: Vector stroke coordinates, colors, shapes, and drawing states created by you.
  • Note Metadata: Note titles, creation/update timestamps, expiration dates, and lock statuses.
  • Passwords / Pins for Locked Notes: When you lock a note, we generate a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash locally. Plaintext passwords are never stored or transmitted.

C. Photos, Media & Camera

  • Images & Attachments: If you attach photos to notes or whiteboard canvases, the app accesses selected images locally.
  • Camera: If you choose to take a photo directly from within the note editor, the camera is used only to capture that specific image.

D. Reminder & Notification Data

  • Scheduled Reminders: Reminder dates, times, and note associations saved locally to trigger system alarms and notifications.

2. How We Store and Process Your Data

A. Local On-Device Storage

  • Room Database (SQLite): Your notes, whiteboard data, and settings are stored locally on your device in the beyond_database database to ensure fast offline access.
  • SharedPreferences: Scheduled reminder histories and basic app state are saved locally on your device (mr_reminder_prefs).

B. Cloud Storage (Google Cloud Firestore)

If you are signed in, your notes and whiteboard data are synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore under an isolated document path (users/{uid}/notes/). Only your authenticated Google account has access to this data.

Note on Firebase Storage: BEYOND does not use Firebase Storage. Image data and vector coordinates are handled locally or structured directly within your Firestore notes collection.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

A. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)

BEYOND displays advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob may collect and process certain data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) and other device identifiers.
  • Coarse Location Data: Derived from your IP address for localized ad serving.
  • Ad Performance & Diagnostic Data: Interaction metrics and viewability data to prevent ad fraud and assess performance.

For more information, please review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

B. Google Firebase

We use Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore for user authentication and cloud synchronization. Learn more at the Firebase Privacy Policy.

C. Google Fonts

The app downloads customizable typography fonts dynamically via the Google Fonts API (fonts.googleapis.com).

D. Analytics & AI Disclaimer

BEYOND does not include third-party analytics trackers (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), crash-reporting trackers (e.g., Crashlytics), or automated AI processing SDKs (e.g., Gemini API, OpenAI).

4. Android Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Cloud note synchronization with Firestore, Google Sign-In, AdMob ad serving, and font loading.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Allows you to select images from your gallery to insert into notes or whiteboard canvases.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Required on older Android versions (≤ Android 9) when exporting notes as PDF files.
CAMERA Optional permission to capture photos directly inside the note editor.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays reminder alarms and task alerts (Android 13+).
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Triggers exact, user-scheduled reminder alarms at the specified date and time.
VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback when a scheduled reminder alarm rings.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedules your active alarms automatically after your device reboots.

5. Data Retention & Security

Security: All network communication between the app and Google Firebase services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.

Retention: We retain your cloud data only as long as you maintain an active account with BEYOND. When you delete a note, it is immediately removed from your local database and your Cloud Firestore subcollection.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app or by request.

How to Delete Your Account In-App:

  1. Open BEYOND on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  3. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog and confirm by tapping Delete Account.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account:

  • Cloud Data: All your notes, whiteboard drawings, and user records under your Firebase UID (users/{uid}) in Google Cloud Firestore are permanently deleted.
  • Local Data: All local notes in the Room database and all reminder preferences in SharedPreferences are completely cleared from your device.
  • Firebase Authentication: Your Firebase Authentication account and session are permanently deleted.
  • Irreversibility: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Request Deletion via Email:

If you cannot access the app, you may request complete deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us at szzada02@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - BEYOND" from the Google account email associated with your BEYOND account. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.
