1. Information We Collect

BEYOND is built with a privacy-first, offline-ready architecture. We only process information required for your direct note-taking, sketching, and organization experience:

A. User Generated Content (Notes, Drawings & QR Codes)

  • Text Notes & Rich Content: All your notes, markdown documents, checklists, drawings, and canvas sketches are saved in the app's secure private sandbox storage on your local device.
  • Biometric Lock Protection: If you lock specific notes with PIN or Fingerprint / Biometric authentication, all biometric checks are executed entirely on-device by the Android Operating System hardware keystore. We never receive, access, or transmit your fingerprint or biometric keys.

B. Account & Cloud Sync Data (Optional)

  • Google Sign-In: You can use BEYOND 100% offline without creating an account. If you voluntarily sign in with Google to enable cross-device cloud sync and backup, we receive your verified Google Account Name, Email Address, and Avatar URL to authenticate your private Firebase Firestore database partition.
  • No Unauthorized Access: We never sell, rent, or share your account information or notes with third-party data brokers.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Anonymous hardware specs (device manufacturer, model, Android OS version) collected by Google Play Services to identify bugs and improve stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and coarse location used by Google AdMob to deliver advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

2. How We Store & Secure Data

  • Local Data Sandboxing: All local notes, voice memos, and canvas sketches are isolated in the app's internal private storage directory, inaccessible to any other applications on your device.
  • Encrypted In-Transit Transmission: Any network communication (such as Firebase Cloud Sync, Google AdMob ad requests, or AI query processing) is strictly encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Zero Unnecessary Server Backdoors: If cloud sync is not enabled, your notes and sketches remain entirely confined to your physical device.

3. Device Permissions Explained

Below is an exhaustive list of every Android permission declared by BEYOND and its exact functional purpose:

android.permission.CAMERA

Required solely when you use the built-in Camera feature to attach fresh photos to your notes or when scanning QR codes directly within the app. Camera frames are processed in real-time in memory and are never uploaded or streamed without your explicit action.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used on Android 13+ to display reminders and scheduled alert notifications for your timed tasks, checklists, and calendar alarms.

android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows BEYOND to trigger exact-time alarms for your note reminders even if your phone is in Doze/standby mode, and restores your active alarm reminders automatically when you restart or reboot your smartphone.

android.permission.VIBRATE

Used to provide tactile haptic feedback during note actions, canvas brush gestures, and notification buzz alerts for your reminders.

android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Enables optional cloud synchronization with Google Firebase, loading AdMob ads, and verifying internet availability before network operations.

Zero-Permission Media Access (Photo Picker)

In full compliance with Google Play's Media and Storage Policy, BEYOND does not request broad storage permissions. Instead, it uses Android's privacy-preserving system Photo Picker, granting the app temporary access only to the exact photos you specifically choose to import.

5. Beyond AI & Generative Services

BEYOND includes an optional AI Assistant ("Beyond AI") to assist users with text summarization, content brainstorming, and creative suggestions:

  • Encrypted Processing: Prompts sent to Beyond AI are transmitted securely over TLS to AI processing endpoints (Google Gemini API).
  • Ephemeral Image Processing: If you attach an image or diagram for AI explanation, the image is processed ephemerally in memory solely to generate your answer and is not retained or used for public training.
  • In-App Content Reporting: In strict compliance with Google Play Generative AI requirements, users can instantly flag or report any inappropriate, biased, or harmful AI response directly from the chat screen using the built-in report option.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

You maintain complete ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Instant Local Deletion: You can delete any note, drawing, or image at any time. When deleted, it is immediately and permanently wiped from local device storage.
  • Cloud Account Deletion Request: If you utilized cloud sync and wish to permanently delete your account, authentication tokens, and all cloud-stored notes from our database, you can email our team at szzada02@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". All associated cloud records will be verified and permanently purged within 30 days.
