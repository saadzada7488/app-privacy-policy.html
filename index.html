Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 2026

Beyond Notes & QR ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Personal Information (Optional Account Creation)

You can use Beyond Notes & QR completely offline without creating an account. If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication to enable cloud backup and synchronization across devices, we collect:

  • Email Address and Name: Used solely to identify your account and authenticate your cloud sync session.
  • Profile Picture URL: Used solely for in-app user avatar display.

B. User Generated Content (Notes, Drawings & QR Codes)

  • Local Storage: Your notes, checklists, canvas drawings, and generated QR codes are stored locally on your device using a private SQLite/Room database.
  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you enable cloud backup, your note data is securely transmitted and stored in your private Google Firebase Firestore database, accessible only with your verified credentials.
  • Biometric Security: If you lock individual notes using Fingerprint / Biometrics, all biometric verification is performed directly by your device's Android OS hardware security module. We never access, store, or transmit your biometric or fingerprint data.

C. Device Identifiers & Analytics (Automatically Collected by Third-Party SDKs)

To serve advertisements and maintain app reliability, our third-party service providers automatically collect standard device information:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads, limit frequency, and combat ad fraud.
  • Crash Logs & Performance Diagnostics: Anonymous technical data such as device model, OS version, and stack traces to fix crashes and improve performance.
  • IP Address: Used by Google AdMob to estimate coarse (city/country level) location for ad serving and fraud prevention.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app integrates third-party SDKs that have their own privacy policies governing data usage:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and interaction data.
    Read Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services: Provides essential background services and API integration.
    Read Google Play Services Policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Provides secure cloud backup and user account authentication.
    Read Firebase Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3. Device Permissions Requested

Our application requests only the permissions necessary to provide core features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose to scan QR codes or capture photos directly for your notes. Video or camera streams are processed locally in real-time and are never transmitted to external servers without your action.
  • Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to deliver local note reminders and scheduled alarm alerts on time.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used locally on device to unlock password-protected notes.
  • Internet & Network State: Required for syncing notes with Firebase and requesting AdMob advertisements.

4. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device, Firebase servers, and AdMob servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Local Sandboxing: Local notes are stored inside the app's private sandbox directory, inaccessible to other apps on your device.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Local Data: You can delete any note, drawing, or QR code at any time from within the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: If you have an active cloud account and wish to delete your synced notes and account information permanently, you can delete your notes in-app or contact our support email at szzada02@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your records within 30 days.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
