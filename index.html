Privacy Policy for Zig Snake

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile game application Zig Snake ("the Application", "the App", or "Game"), developed and published by Saad Zada ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

Summary: Zig Snake does not collect or request any personal identification information (such as your real name, phone number, physical address, or contacts). All core gameplay progress is saved locally on your device. We use Google AdMob solely for in-game advertisements and rewarded gameplay features.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) directly from players. When you play the Game, we do not require you to create an account, register, or provide private details.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Identifiers (Google AdMob)

The Application integrates Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device and usage data to serve relevant advertisements, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google Play Services to personalize or cap the frequency of advertisements.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding whether an advertisement was loaded, clicked, or completed (e.g., watching a rewarded ad to revive or earn in-game coins).

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Local Storage of Game Data

Your high scores, collected coins, earned diamonds, and unlocked snake skins are stored locally on your device using secure application internal storage. We do not transmit or host your gameplay scores on an external server. Please note that clearing the game's cache/data or uninstalling the app may reset your local progress.

4. Children’s Privacy

Zig Snake is built for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that non-compliant data has been inadvertently collected through third-party ad networks, please contact us immediately so we can address the concern.

5. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not store user accounts or personal profiles on remote servers, we do not retain your personal data. All network communication with Google ad delivery systems is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS/SSL) protocols.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or compliance requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
